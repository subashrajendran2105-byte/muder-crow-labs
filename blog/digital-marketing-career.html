MARKETING CAREER

How to start a digital marketing career when you are a beginner.

Murder Crow Learn · Updated for 2026

If you are searching “digital marketing jobs” or “how to get a digital marketing job”, it is easy to focus only on certificates. A better approach is to build a small stack of skills and show what you can actually do.

Pick a practical starting area

You do not need to master every marketing channel at once. Start with a useful combination such as social media + content, SEO + content, paid ads + analytics, or marketing + AI workflows.

Build projects

Create a sample campaign for a real or imaginary business. Audit a website. Build a content calendar. Write an SEO article. Design a landing page. Analyse an advertising example. Create a CRM follow-up flow. Explain your decisions.

Build a portfolio

Your portfolio should show the problem, your thinking, the work you created and what you would measure. A polished collection of random graphics is less useful than a few well-explained marketing projects.

Learn tools as you need them

Learn the fundamentals first, then use tools to execute. Tools change. Marketing principles, customer understanding and analytical thinking remain useful.

Prepare for interviews

  • Be able to explain a campaign you built.
  • Know basic marketing metrics.
  • Understand the difference between reach, clicks, leads and conversions.
  • Explain how you would research a new audience.
  • Show that you can learn unfamiliar tools.
  • Talk clearly about mistakes and what you would improve.
Job-ready does not mean knowing everything. It means being able to learn, execute, explain your decisions and improve from evidence.

Can AI help?

Yes. AI can accelerate research, ideation, content, analysis and repetitive workflows. Learn to use it responsibly and verify important information.
