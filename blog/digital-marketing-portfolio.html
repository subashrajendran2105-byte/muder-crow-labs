CAREER · PORTFOLIO

Your digital marketing portfolio should prove how you think.

Murder Crow Learn · Updated for 2026

A beginner digital marketing portfolio does not need twenty projects. It needs a few pieces of work that clearly show your ability to understand a problem, choose a strategy, execute and measure.

Project idea 1: Website and SEO audit

Choose a business website. Identify its audience, search intent, page structure, content gaps, technical issues and opportunities. Give practical recommendations.

Project idea 2: Social media strategy

Create a 30-day plan with audience segments, content pillars, formats, hooks, distribution and metrics. Explain why each content type exists.

Project idea 3: Paid campaign plan

Build a sample Google Ads or Meta Ads structure with audience, creative concepts, landing page, budget logic, tracking and success metrics.

Project idea 4: Growth experiment

Choose one conversion problem and write a hypothesis. Define the change, audience, metric, expected result and how you would decide what to do next.

Project idea 5: AI marketing workflow

Show how you would use an AI tool for research, content variation or analysis. Include your prompt, inputs, checks and final human-edited result.

How to present each case study

  • Problem: what were you trying to solve?
  • Audience: who mattered?
  • Insight: what did you learn?
  • Strategy: what did you decide?
  • Execution: what did you make?
  • Measurement: what would you track?
  • Reflection: what would you improve?
Remember: a portfolio is not a gallery. It is evidence of your thinking.

Keep it honest

If a project is a simulation, label it as a simulation. If you worked with a real business, explain your role accurately. Strong portfolios build trust rather than exaggerating results.
