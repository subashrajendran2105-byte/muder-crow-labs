SOCIAL MEDIA MARKETING

Social media marketing is more than posting every day.

Murder Crow Learn · Updated for 2026

Social media marketing is the use of social platforms to attract attention, communicate with an audience, build trust, create demand and drive action. Posting frequently can be part of the work, but it is not the whole strategy.

Start with the audience

Before choosing a content format, ask who you are trying to reach, what they care about, what problem they have and what would make them stop scrolling.

Build content around a purpose

  • Education: teach something useful.
  • Proof: show results, projects, testimonials or process.
  • Perspective: share an opinion or framework.
  • Entertainment: create attention and memorability.
  • Conversion: give people a clear next step.

Organic social and paid social

Organic content can build familiarity and community. Paid social can help distribute specific messages to targeted audiences. Strong marketing often uses both, depending on the goal.

What should you measure?

Do not stop at likes. Depending on the objective, useful metrics can include reach, watch time, saves, clicks, leads, conversion rate, cost per lead and revenue.

Better question: instead of asking “What should I post today?”, ask “What does my audience need to understand next?”

Where does AI fit?

AI tools can help generate ideas, variations, scripts, captions, visual concepts and content repurposing. The strategy and final editorial judgement should remain human-led.
