GROWTH MARKETING

What is growth marketing?

Murder Crow Learn · Updated for 2026

Growth marketing is a way of looking at the whole customer journey rather than treating every marketing channel as a separate activity. The question is not only “how do we get attention?” but also “what happens after attention?”

Simple model: attract → activate → convert → retain → learn → improve.

How growth marketing works

Acquisition

How do people discover you? Search, social media, advertising, partnerships, referrals and content can all contribute.

Activation

Does the person quickly understand the value of the product, service or programme? Landing pages, onboarding and messaging matter here.

Conversion

What makes someone take the next step? The offer, proof, friction, price, trust and user experience all influence conversion.

Retention

Do people continue using the product or service? Retention is where many growth systems become much more valuable than a one-time acquisition campaign.

Experimentation

Growth teams form a hypothesis, change something, measure the result and learn. Not every experiment wins. A failed test can still teach you what not to repeat.

Growth marketing vs digital marketing

Digital marketing usually describes marketing through digital channels. Growth marketing is more system-oriented and often combines marketing, product, analytics, experimentation, CRM and customer behaviour. There is a lot of overlap.

What skills does a growth marketer need?

  • Customer and market research
  • Messaging and positioning
  • Content and creative thinking
  • SEO and paid acquisition
  • Analytics and experimentation
  • Funnels and conversion optimisation
  • CRM and automation
  • AI tools and workflow design
  • Clear communication and business thinking

Why learn growth marketing?

Because modern marketing rarely works as one isolated channel. A growth marketer learns to connect the pieces and ask better questions about the system.
