AI tools for marketers: learn the categories, not just the names.

Murder Crow Learn · Updated for 2026

There are more AI tools appearing every week. If you search “best AI tools” you can quickly end up with a giant list. A better approach is to understand what job you need the tool to do.

1. AI for research

Use AI to organise information, summarise supplied material, compare ideas and generate research questions. Always verify important facts against reliable sources.

2. AI for writing

AI can help with outlines, drafts, variations, rewrites, hooks, email ideas and content repurposing. Your brand voice, facts and final judgement still matter.

3. AI for design and images

Image generation and editing tools can accelerate concepts, moodboards, ad variations and visual exploration. They are especially useful when a marketer understands composition and communication.

4. AI for video and audio

AI can assist with scripts, captions, voice, editing and creative variations. The best result still starts with a strong idea and audience understanding.

5. AI for SEO

AI can help brainstorm topics, structure content, identify questions to answer and speed up repetitive work. It should not be used to mass-produce low-value pages.

6. AI for analytics

AI can help explain datasets, surface patterns and turn numbers into questions. Make sure the underlying data is correct and that important decisions are checked.

7. AI for automation

AI becomes especially useful when connected to workflows: capturing a lead, classifying an enquiry, drafting a response, updating a CRM or triggering a follow-up.

Best rule: do not ask “Which AI tool should I learn?” first. Ask “What marketing task do I want to do better?” Then choose the tool.

What should a beginner learn?

Start with one general AI assistant, one image or design workflow, one research workflow and one automation workflow. Learn prompting, context, verification and evaluation before collecting more apps.
