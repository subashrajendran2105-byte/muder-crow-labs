AI Tools for Marketing: What they do and how to use them.

AI tools can help with research, writing, analysis, ideas, automation and repetitive work. The useful skill is knowing what to ask, what to verify and where AI should not replace human judgement.

How AI tools work in simple terms

Most generative AI tools take an input, process it through a trained model and return a prediction or generated output. The quality of the result depends on the task, context, instructions, data and human review.

Where marketers can use AI

  • Research and idea generation
  • Content outlines and drafts
  • Social media planning
  • Ad creative variations
  • SEO content research
  • Customer and competitor analysis
  • Reporting and summarisation
  • Workflow automation
AI is a tool, not the strategy.
Start with the business problem. Then choose the AI tool that helps solve that problem.

What should you learn first?

Start with prompting, verification, structured research, content workflows, data interpretation and simple automation. Then move into specialised tools for your role.
