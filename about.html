ABOUT MURDER CROW · #LABS

A murder is a group of crows.
We became one.

Not a company built around a classroom. A collective built around people figuring things out — learning, building, failing, improving and helping the next person rise.

01 · WHY WE EXIST

We don't just teach skills. We build people who can figure things out.

Murder Crow #Labs brings strategy, creativity, digital marketing, technology, AI and career thinking to the same table — with practical work instead of empty theory.

THE IDEA

Learn. Build. Fail. Repeat.

The real world doesn't hand you a syllabus. It gives you problems. We want people to become confident enough to solve them.

THE COLLECTIVE

Grow together.

A murder of crows is a group. That became our metaphor: individual minds becoming stronger when they share what they know.

03 · THE OATH

When I grow, someone grows with me.

As the community grows, we want learning to reach people who normally couldn't afford it. The long-term goal is to create opportunities for students who need the chance most — and eventually help fund education for kids who cannot afford it.

04 · WHAT MURDER CROW MEANS

Not the strongest bird. The smartest group.

Crows are known for intelligence, memory and community. That's the spirit behind Murder Crow: use what you learn, remember what you've been through, and bring someone else with you.

For the curious.

For people changing careers, upgrading skills, starting again or simply refusing to stop learning.

For the next generation.

Our goal is bigger than a course: build a community that can eventually create learning opportunities for kids who cannot afford them.
