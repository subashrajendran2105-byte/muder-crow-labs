03 · THE OATH

When I grow, someone grows with me.

As the community grows, we want learning to reach people who normally couldn't afford it. Our vision includes practical learning opportunities for underprivileged people, struggling creatives and young people. We also want to encourage mental-health awareness, reduce stigma around seeking help and remind people they are not limited to one path.

04 · WHAT MURDER CROW MEANS

Crow will emerge one day.

Crows are known for intelligence, memory and community. That's the spirit behind Murder Crow: use what you learn, remember what you've been through, and bring someone else with you.

For the curious.

For people changing careers, upgrading skills, starting again or simply refusing to stop learning.

For the next generation.

Our goal is bigger than a course: build a community that can eventually create learning opportunities for kids who cannot afford them.
