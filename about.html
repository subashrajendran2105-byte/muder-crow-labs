ABOUT MURDER CROW #LABS

A murder of crows.
A collective of creative minds.

“Murder” is the English collective noun commonly used for a group of crows. We simplified that idea into Murder Crow — a name for a group of creative, curious people who think together, build together and keep learning.

🪶
WHY THE CROW?

The bird people judge too quickly.

Crows can mean different things to different people. Some communities respect or even worship them in connection with ancestors and remembrance; others see a crow as a bad sign. We are interested in that contradiction.

We use the crow as a symbol of people and ideas that are sometimes judged before they are understood. Murder Crow stands for curiosity, adaptability, creative intelligence and the courage to question those assumptions.

✦
OUR BELIEF

The most interesting minds don't always fit the usual box.

Creators, marketers, designers, developers, strategists, filmmakers, founders and learners can become much stronger when they share what they know.

Murder Crow is meant to be a collective — not a room full of people pretending to have everything figured out.

THE IDEA IN ONE LINE
Don't just build your career.
Build the people around you.

We want learning to move from person to person. Someone learns a skill here, applies it, grows and eventually helps another person learn. That is the culture we want to build.
