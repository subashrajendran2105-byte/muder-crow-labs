About Murder Crow

Not just a brand.
A collective.

Murder Crow is our idea of a group of creative people coming together to learn, build, experiment, share what they know and help each other move forward.

🐦‍⬛
Why the name?

A murder is a group of crows.

We took that unusual name and gave it our own meaning. A creative person can be powerful alone. A group of curious, skilled and generous people can build something much bigger. Murder Crow is that collective idea.

What we believe

Learn the skill.
Understand the system.

Don't just collect tools.

Tools change. Fundamentals stay useful. We want people to understand why the work works, then use modern tools to make the work faster and better.

Build proof, not just certificates.

Practical projects, portfolios, experiments and real understanding give learners something they can actually show.

Keep learning together.

The digital world changes quickly. A collective makes it easier to share discoveries, mistakes, useful tools and opportunities.

Help someone else climb.

Our learning model is designed around growth that can travel beyond one person — learn, become stronger, and help another person get the chance to learn too.
