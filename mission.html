Murder Crow #Labs · Our Mission

Learn. Grow. Give someone else a chance.

We are building Murder Crow as more than a learning programme — a growing community of people who want to develop their skills, build careers and eventually create opportunities for others.

THE IDEA

One person learning can help another person learn.

Not everyone starts with the same access to education, technology, mentors or career guidance. As Murder Crow grows, we want our learning community to become a way of creating more access for people who have not had that privilege.

THE FIRST MILESTONE
100

Students first.

Our first goal is to build a community of 100 learners. As we reach milestones, we want to turn that growth into support for learners who cannot comfortably afford the same opportunities.

01 · LEARN

Build your own skills.

Learn digital marketing, growth, AI tools, creative thinking and practical systems you can use in real work.

02 · GROW

Build your career.

Use the skills, projects and guidance to become more confident about your next career move. Outcomes always depend on your own effort and circumstances.

03 · GIVE BACK

Create another opportunity.

As Murder Crow grows, our long-term goal is to sponsor learning opportunities for people who cannot afford them — and eventually support education initiatives for children from underserved communities.

The ₹10 isn't the whole story.
It's where the journey begins.

BUILD WITH US

Learn with us today. Build with us tomorrow.

Murder Crow is also meant to become a place for people who figure things out, make things, experiment and solve problems. As the community develops, there may be opportunities to work with us, collaborate on projects or help others learn.

A BIGGER DREAM

Skills should not belong only to people who can afford them.

Our long-term ambition is to grow a sustainable business that can also fund meaningful education and skill-building initiatives. We will announce specific programmes and sponsorship commitments as each milestone is reached.
